    How the figures are calculated

    Connecticut sales tax. Vehicles priced over $50,000 are taxed at 7.75% on the whole price, not just the part over $50k. The $50,000 test uses the price before any trade-in. A trade-in at the selling dealer lowers the taxable amount, so each $1 of trade-in saves 7.75¢ in tax.

    Break-even. Trading a car in beats selling it privately once the trade offer is at least the private price ÷ 1.0775, or about 93% of it.

    Not included. Loan payoffs, financing costs, and offers from Carvana or CarMax. An outright sale to them gets no tax credit, so compare it to the private price, not the trade-in.

    Starting values come from November 2026 listings and KBB/J.D. Power ranges for comparable vehicles, adjusted for condition. They're estimates, not appraisals.
