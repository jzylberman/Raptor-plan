    🤓 How the math works

    CT sales tax. Vehicles priced over $50,000 are taxed at 7.75% on the whole price, not just the part over $50k. The $50k test uses the price before any trade-in. Trade-ins at the selling dealer lower the taxable amount, so each $1 of trade-in saves 7.75¢ in tax.

    Break-even. Trading in a car beats selling it privately once the trade offer is at least the private price ÷ 1.0775 (about 93% of the private price).

    Not included. Loan payoffs, financing costs, and offers from Carvana or CarMax. An outright sale to them gets no tax credit, so compare it to the private price, not the trade-in.

    Starting values come from Nov 2026 market listings and KBB/J.D. Power ranges for similar vehicles. They're estimates, not appraisals.
